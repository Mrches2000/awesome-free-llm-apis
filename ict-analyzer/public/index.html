This is an analysis aid, not financial advice. It applies ICT rules mechanically and cannot know your account, your broker's prices or what happens next. Futures are leveraged — you can lose more than you expect. Check every level on your own chart before placing an order.

Add your chart and press Analyze

Choose the market at the top, drop in a screenshot of your chart, and the AI reads the candles and the indicators on it. For gold and Nasdaq futures the exact ICT levels are then calculated from price data for the same market. Or switch Data to live prices: the engine reads Daily → H4 → H1 → M15 → M5 → M1, maps liquidity, fair value gaps, order blocks, breakers, premium/discount, killzones and SMT, then scores the best trade.
