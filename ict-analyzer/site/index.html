Every session

  1. Check the news calendar. No trade 30 minutes before red-folder USD news.
  2. Screenshot the Daily or H1 chart for the bias.
  3. Wait for a killzone.
  4. Screenshot M5 or M1 and press Analyze.
  5. Trade only a LONG or SHORT. WAIT means wait.
  6. Half off at 1.5R, stop to break-even. Log it in the Journal.

Killzones New York · your time

A trade needs all of these

  • Score 65 or more
  • Raid, then shift, then a gap or block
  • At least 1.5R of room to the target
  • Long in discount, short in premium
  • Same side as the bias
  • No news within 30 minutes · not lunch

Words

Liquidity
Stops resting above old highs and below old lows.
Sweep
Price takes those stops, then closes back inside.
MSS
Structure shift: a strong close through the last swing the other way.
FVG
Gap left by a fast move. Price often returns to it.
Order block
Last opposite candle before the move.
Premium / discount
Upper and lower half of the range.
OTE
A pullback of 62% to 79%.
R
Your risk. 2R is twice what you risked.
