The routine, every session

  1. Check the calendar. No new trade within 30 minutes before CPI, NFP, FOMC or any other red-folder USD release.
  2. Get the bias. Screenshot the Daily or H1 chart. Know which side the market is drawn to before you look at a 5-minute chart.
  3. Wait for a killzone. London, New York AM or New York PM. Never NY lunch.
  4. Screenshot the entry chart (M5 or M1) and press Analyze. Add the higher-timeframe picture with it.
  5. Trade only a LONG or SHORT verdict. WAIT means wait. The page tells you what has to happen first.
  6. Place entry and stop exactly as given, moved to the real candle high or low on your chart. Size the position from your dollar risk.
  7. Manage it. Take part off at 1.5R and move the stop to break-even. Let the rest run to the ★ target.
  8. Log the result in the Journal the same day: win, loss or break-even, and one line on why.

When the page says LONG or SHORT

  • The score is 65 or more out of 100 (B from 65, A from 73, A+ from 82).
  • Liquidity was raided, structure shifted, and there is an entry gap or block.
  • The liquidity target is at least 1.5R away from the entry.
  • A long is entered in discount, a short in premium.
  • The trade agrees with the bias.
  • No high-impact news in the next 30 minutes, not NY lunch, not the 16:00 to 18:00 close.

ICT words in plain language

Liquidity
Stop orders resting above old highs (buy-side) and below old lows (sell-side). Price is drawn to them.
Sweep / raid
Price pokes through an old high or low to take those stops, then closes back inside.
MSS
Market structure shift. After a raid, a candle closes through the last swing the other way, with force.
Displacement
A strong, fast move that leaves a gap behind it.
FVG
Fair value gap. Three candles where the first and third do not overlap. Price often returns to it.
Order block
The last opposite-colour candle before the move that broke structure.
Breaker
An order block that failed and now works the other way.
Premium / discount
Upper half and lower half of the current range. Buy in discount, sell in premium.
OTE
Optimal trade entry. A pullback of 62% to 79% of the move.
Killzone
The hours when the big moves usually start.
R
Your risk on the trade. 2R means the profit is twice what you risked.

All of the code

The complete system, stored privately with this site. The algorithm is in lib/ict.js (detectors) and lib/analyze.js (bias, liquidity, entry model, score, trade plan). lib/vision.js reads your screenshot and checks it. To run everything on your own computer: save the files, then node server.js.
